## Rules that apply to every image

These keep the site legally clean and stop the pictures reading as AI:

1. **No text, no logos, no brand names, anywhere in frame.** Not on clothing,
   not on equipment, not on signage. A visible competitor logo or a fake
   Bluepeak logo both cause problems.
2. **No legible screens.** Any TV, laptop or tablet in shot must show blurred,
   abstract or out-of-focus content. Recognisable shows or UI create rights
   issues, and a visible speed-test number would be an unverifiable claim.
3. **Ordinary-looking people, not models.** Real body shapes, real clothes,
   natural skin with visible texture. Nobody looking into the camera.
4. **Lived-in rooms.** A little clutter, a worn cushion, a cable, a mug. Spotless
   showroom interiors are the single biggest AI tell.
5. **Midwest / Plains feel** — the footprint is North Dakota, South Dakota,
   Minnesota, Oklahoma, Texas and Wyoming. Modest single-family homes, not
   coastal lofts or glass condos.

### Append this to every prompt

```
Shot on a Canon EOS R6 with a 35mm f/1.8 lens, available natural light only, no
flash. Candid documentary style, unposed, nobody looking at the camera. Natural
skin texture with visible pores and stray hair, no retouching, no beauty
smoothing. Muted realistic colour grading, slightly lifted blacks, fine film
grain. Shallow depth of field with a naturally soft background. Imperfect
framing as if taken quickly. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks,
no legible screen content.
```

**Avoid these words** — they all pull the model toward the glossy AI look:
`beautiful`, `stunning`, `perfect`, `hyperrealistic`, `8k`, `ultra detailed`,
`professional photography`, `cinematic lighting`, `masterpiece`.

## What happens once you deliver

1. Images go in via `next/image`, so each one is served as WebP/AVIF at the size
   the device actually needs.
2. The hero image gets `priority` and a blur placeholder, so it does not cost
   anything on Largest Contentful Paint. Every other image is lazy-loaded.
3. Each gets descriptive `alt` text for screen readers and SEO.
4. `og-share.jpg` gets wired into the metadata in `app/layout.tsx`.
5. I'll re-run the build and re-check that there is still zero horizontal
   overflow at 320px.

**If you send only some of them, that is fine** — I'll wire up whatever arrives
and leave the other sections as they are today.
